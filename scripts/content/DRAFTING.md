# Drafting guide (per band)

Write short, natural, situational sentences a learner at this band would actually say or hear.

| band | words/sentence | tense & structures | tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| pre | 2–6 | present, fixed phrases | greetings, yes/no, numbers |
| A1 | 3–8 | present; simple questions; «I have / I want / there is» | everyday survival |
| A2 | 4–10 | past (perfect), future with modal/going-to, comparatives, simple connectors (because, but, when) | daily life, plans, opinions |
| B1 | 6–12 | narrative past, conditionals, reported speech, relative clauses | experiences, work, feelings |
| B2 | 8–14 | passive, nuance, idioms in context | argument, media, society |
| C1 | 8–14 | register shifts, precise collocations | abstract topics |

Rules
1. Each sentence covers 1–3 words from the gap list; write them in `targetWords` exactly as listed.
2. Only ONE word per sentence may be above the band's frequency ceiling.
3. Persian (`fa`): natural, same register, no literal word order; no transliteration.
4. `pattern`: the reusable frame, with the slot in angle brackets, e.g. `Ich habe <Akk.> gekauft.`
5. `lesson`: set when the sentence clearly belongs to a shipped lesson (same situation); otherwise leave
   empty — the app attaches it by word overlap.
6. No duplicates of shipped sentences; vary the subject/object so the pattern, not the sentence, is learned.
